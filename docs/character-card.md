## 0. The soul in one paragraph

*A WingXAI monster is a Prototype Frame: a monumental, carbon-fiber-armored machine with huge mechanical wings, LED accent lighting in red or green, and an original gold crest. It's built like a monument and lit like a weapon system. Each frame is presented on the WingXAI frame card, a classified dossier with a unit code, class, systems checklist, quote, and barcode. Every frame and every pilot is fictional.*

**Three words:** *carbon · crested · lit*
**Never:** real people's names, likenesses, or handles; real companies or brands as affiliations; real government seals; copies of existing mecha or anime characters; rust or salvaged wear; cute toy plastic; organic flesh; rainbow or neon palettes.

## 1. Visual DNA

### 1.0 Where this style came from

The style was distilled from a private mood board. It held the owner's own Prototype Frame art and blank frame template, plus outside examples that included real people credited as pilots, real company affiliations, seal-like emblems, and designs that closely follow a well-known copyrighted mecha franchise.

This card keeps only the transferable visual language and the owner's original template. It drops every real name, handle, company affiliation, seal, and franchise design. The mood board images aren't part of the card and shouldn't be shared as WingXAI art or style references. See section 1.9 for how to work from references the right way.

- Lights take about 3–8% of the image, enough to read on a phone, not enough to wash out the armor.

### 1.4 Proportions and silhouette

- **Stance:** monumental and heroic. Upright, chest out, often seen from a low angle like a statue.
- **Body:** broad armored chest and shoulders that frame a central crest. [Head and limb ratios from the owner's frames: ________]
- **Head:** helmet-like, with a narrow visor or slit eyes. No mouth or soft facial features.
- **Head crest (optional):** a forehead fin or antenna is allowed, but it must be an original shape (see 1.6).
- **Wings (family signature):** large, mechanical, with carbon fiber texture. As wide as the body or wider, readable in silhouette, spread or half-spread. Light-up wing edges are encouraged.
- **Paneling:** clean, hard-edged geometric plates with crisp seams. Precise and engineered, not battle-worn.
- **Silhouette test:** filled solid black, it should read as *upright, winged, and monumental*, and should not be mistaken for any existing named mecha.

### 1.5 Texture language

- **Primary:** carbon fiber twill weave on the body and wings, visible at normal viewing distance. On Parade frames, carbon shows on joints, the inner frame, and wing struts.
- **Secondary:** matte to satin plates.
- **Heraldry:** polished brass with engraved detail and slight wear in the recesses.
- **Light:** LED strips cast thin rim highlights in their own color on nearby edges.
- **Condition:** new and pristine, a prototype just unveiled. [Battle-damage variants allowed? ________]
- **Rendering:** [cinematic 3D render / hyperreal concept art / ________]

### 1.6 Mecha influence rule (required)

Some mood board images follow a famous copyrighted mobile-suit design closely. WingXAI treats classic mecha anime as a **general style influence only**.

**OK to draw on (general genre language):**
- Tall, armored mobile-suit proportions
- White-and-gold or white-and-red armor schemes
- A forehead fin or antenna as a general idea
- Mechanical wing language: layered feather-blades, struts, thrusters
- Hard-edged panel lines and vents

**Not OK (hard rule):**
- Reproducing or closely tracing any existing named character or mecha design, including its exact head, face mask, chest vents, wing shape, or overall layout
- Franchise names, model numbers, unit names, or series designations from existing works, in the art, on the HUD, or in prompts
- Prompting for an existing franchise or character by name (no "in the style of [franchise]", "[character name] but…")
- Using a franchise image as a direct image-to-image source

**Make it WingXAI:** every frame should carry the family signatures that franchise designs don't have, which are carbon fiber weave, red or green LED strips, and an original gold crest. Change the head design, wing shape, and chest layout so they're clearly your own.
- [Owner's list of signature head and wing shapes, once drawn: ________]

### 1.7 Heraldry rule (required)

The heraldry is inspired by American civic and military crest styles. **Every WingXAI crest must be an original design.** This is a hard rule.

- **Never reproduce** the Seal of the President, the Great Seal of the United States, the Vice President's seal, or any agency, military, or state seal or insignia. US law restricts their use (for example, 18 U.S.C. § 713 covers the presidential and vice-presidential seals), and they can imply official endorsement.
- **Circular shoulder or chest emblems are fine only if original.** A round badge with an eagle, a ring of stars, and lettering is exactly the layout that reads as an official seal, so avoid that combination. Use your own shield shape, a stylized mechanical eagle, abstract star clusters, laurel or wing motifs, and WingXAI lettering.
- **No real seal text or mottos,** and no real company or agency logos.
- **Distinctness check:** placed next to any real seal, the crest should read as clearly different at a glance in shape, layout, and text.

## 4. Style modifiers

Each mode changes **mood and detail only**. The wings, LED lighting, original crest, and carbon fiber always stay.

| Mode | What bends | Example prompt |
|---|---|---|
| **Cute** | Compact rounder chassis, bigger helmet, stubby folded wings, soft glow | `[base prompt], compact chibi proportions, oversized helmet, small folded carbon fiber wings with soft green edge lights, tiny original gold eagle badge, playful salute pose, soft studio lighting` |
| **Menacing** | Extreme low angle, wings fully spread, bright seams, smoke and sparks | `[base prompt], towering extreme low-angle view, wings fully spread like a blade fan, blazing red visor, red light pouring from every seam, smoke and sparks, storm sky` |
| **Minimal** | Flat shapes, 4–5 colors, no texture noise | `[WingXAI style] minimal flat vector original robot frame, bold geometric silhouette, large angular wings, black and charcoal flat fills, green eye slit, simple original gold eagle emblem, plain dark background` |
| **Detailed** | Macro weave, engraved crest, dense card | `[base prompt], ultra-detailed carbon fiber twill, engraved original gold eagle crest with laurel and stars, green LED wing veins, red LED chest seams, layered panels, 8k` |
| **Parade** | White-and-gold armor, ceremonial pose | `[base prompt with Parade scheme], white armor plates with gold trim, carbon fiber joints, original angular forehead fin, layered blade-feather wings with green edge lights, ceremonial stance` |
| **Gallery card** | Frame card on, centered hero | `[base prompt], [frame card add-on], centered full-body hero pose, generous margins` |

**Dial cheatsheet**
- Cuteness: helmet-to-body ratio and glow softness
- Threat: camera angle, wingspan, and how much light is showing
- Complexity: card density and heraldic detail

## 5. Using references the right way

Mood board images teach the **language** of the style. They are not pictures to copy.

**Take from references**
- Texture: how the carbon weave catches light, how plates meet.
- Lighting: thin LED strips, rim light, dark backgrounds.
- Color: the role and rough share of each color.
- Composition: low camera angle, centered heroic pose, wing spread, card framing.

**Don't take from references**
- The specific figure, pose, or layout of any single image.
- Any real person, name, handle, quote, or pilot credit.
- Real company names, logos, products, or affiliations.
- Seals, insignia, franchise logos, model numbers, or unit names.
- Designs belonging to an existing franchise character (see 1.6).

**Practical rules**
1. Describe the style in words, as this card does, and generate from the words. Don't feed a single reference in as a direct image-to-image source.
2. If you use image references at all, use several at once at low strength so no single image dominates. [Team default for reference strength: ________]
3. Change at least three things from any reference: pose, head design, wing shape, crest design, camera angle, or card contents.
4. Side-by-side test: put your frame next to the closest reference. If someone would call it a copy or edit of that image or of a known franchise character, rework it.
5. Only use reference images you have the right to use, and don't post mood board images as WingXAI art.

### 6.4 Agent recipe: generate on-style

1. Call `get_style_card` and take the base prompt, negative prompt, the mecha influence and heraldry rules, the frame card layout, and the mode modifiers.
2. Optionally call `search_monsters` for 2–3 reference frames in the target mode, scheme, or light.
3. Build the prompt as base prompt + scheme + light + mode modifier + the frame-specific idea. Add the frame card add-on for gallery posts.
4. Fill the frame card with fictional values only.
5. Never add a real person, real company, real seal, or franchise name or character to the positive prompt or the card.
6. Self-check against the checklist in section 8 before publishing.
